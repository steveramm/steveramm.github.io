A warm welcome to Elena Breitenberger, a Masters students from the Biodiversité, Ecologie et Evolution programme at Université de Bordeaux, who is joining the lab for the first half of 2024 for her 6-month M2 internship. Elena's project will be using meta-analysis to investigate multi-stressor impacts on animal fertility, expanding our recent meta-analysis of temperature effects on fertility to now incorporate the effects of two important additional stressors, salinity and pH. The project contributes to the [ESEB STN on The Evolutionary Ecology of Thermal Fertility Limits](https://thermal-fertility-network-eseb.com) and is being conducted in collaboration with [Liam Dougherty](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/infection-veterinary-and-ecological-sciences/staff/liam-dougherty/) in Liverpool and [Natalie Pilakouta](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/biology/people/np84/) and Amber Chatten in St. Andrews.
