Thanks and best wishes to Michael Weber, leaving the lab after six years as a PhD student and postdoc. Michael was instrumental (along with former PhD student [Bahar](https://baharpatlar.wixsite.com/bpatlar)) in developing _Macrostomum_ as a model system for studying seminal fluid-mediated effects, and has more recently been investigating environmental impacts on fertility.
