title: "Emma Lavaut joins the lab"
date: 2024-09-23
---

A warm welcome to Emma Lavaut who has joined ECOBIO as an "LRU" (temporary lecturer-researcher). Based on Emma's PhD experience working on sexual selection in brown algae (see e.g. [here](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abo6661) and [here](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rspb.2023.1668)), we are developing a join project on investigating how environmental stress impacts Bateman gradients and other sexual selection metrics in _Macrostomum_.
