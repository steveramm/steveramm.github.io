It was a great pleasure to welcome [Jean-François Lemaître](http://lbbe.univ-lyon1.fr/-Lemaitre-Jean-Francois-.html?lang=fr) to Bielefeld as the guest speaker in our Behaviour & Evolution Seminar Series this week, in which he talked about "Understanding sex-differences in ageing: insights from natural populations of mammals". Jean-François is a CNRS researcher based at the [Laboratoire de Biométrie et Biologie Évolutive](http://lbbe.univ-lyon1.fr/) in Lyon, where he works on the evolutionary demography of wild mammals. Thanks for your visit, Jeff!
