Congratulations to lead author Yumi Nakadera on [this study](https://doi.org/10.1111/jeb.13683) recently published in _Journal of Evolutionary Biology_. As part of her Alexander von Humboldt postdoc fellowship here in Bielefeld, Yumi sampled four different wild populations of _Lymnaea stagnalis_ pond snails spread across Germany, the Netherlands and France, and then kept them under common garden conditions in the lab to examine natural divergence in seminal fluid expression and function. We found evidence for divergence in both the expression of key seminal fluid proteins and the post-mating responses that seminal fluid induces in mating partners. This was a nice collaboration with colleagues from Amsterdam and Rennes, and Yumi is now continuing to work on related questions as a postdoc in Amsterdam.
