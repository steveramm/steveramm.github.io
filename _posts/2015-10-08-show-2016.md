[![SHOW-2016-Logo]({{ '/assets/images/show-2016/show-2016-logo.jpg' | relative_url }})]({{ '/assets/images/show-2016/show-2016-logo.jpg' | relative_url }}) The Simultaneously Hermaphroditic Organisms Workshop is an annual meeting of researchers working on questions related to sex in hermaphrodites. We’re proud to be hosting the next instalment, at Bielefeld University’s Centre for Interdisciplinary Research (ZiF) from 17-19 February 2016. The workshop will be attended by members of the established SHOW network from across Europe, but we’re always open to new participants too. So, if you work on sex in hermaphrodites (animals or plants) and would be interested in joining the meeting, or would like more info, please get in touch.
