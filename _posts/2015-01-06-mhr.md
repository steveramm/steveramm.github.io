I'm pleased to be joining the Editorial Board of [_Molecular Human Reproduction_](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org) this year as an Associate Editor. One of three journals run by ESHRE (the European Society of Human Reproduction and Embryology), _MHR_ focuses on the basic science of reproduction, including in non-human model systems. I recently worked with the journal to produce this [Special Issue](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org/content/20/12.toc) on sperm competition.
