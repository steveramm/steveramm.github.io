Welcome back to [Bahar](http://www.uni-bielefeld.de/\(en\)/biologie/Evolutionsbiologie/mitarbeiter/patlar.html), just returned from a productive research trip to the [Genetic Diversity Centre](http://www.gdc.ethz.ch) at the ETH Zürich. We're working with the GDC to measure variation in seminal fluid gene expression in _Macrostomum lignano_, as part of our [DFG project](http://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/240721607) on seminal fluid function and evolution.
