After our move from Bielefeld, the research group in Rennes is now officially up and running with the arrival of our first two PhD students. Both projects will develop our broad goal of understanding environmental influences on fertility, with Sarthak's project more focussed on mating system variation and Elise studying plastic and evolutionary fertility responses to shifts in thermal and salinity regimes. Welcome both!
