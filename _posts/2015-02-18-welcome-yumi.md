We're delighted to welcome Dr. Yumi Nakadera to the group this week. Yumi is joining us as a postdoc having been awarded funding from the Young Researchers' Fund of Bielefeld University to develop her research programme on seminal fluid-mediated effects in snails; she recently completed a Ph.D. on this topic in the group of [Joris Koene](http://www.joriskoene.com) at VU University Amsterdam.
