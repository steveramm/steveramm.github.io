Congratulations to [Michael](http://www.uni-bielefeld.de/\(en\)/biologie/Evolutionsbiologie/mitarbeiter/weber.html) on submitting his PhD thesis on the identification and functional characterisation of seminal fluid proteins in _Macrostomum_!

Michael's and Bahar's projects were tackling the evolutionary genetics of seminal fluid from complementary perspectives; together they begin to build a fascinating picture of the role seminal fluid plays in flatworm reproduction, and I think firmly establish _M. lignano_ as a neat model system for studying seminal fluid-mediated effects more generally. They have already published the [first papers](https://macrostomum.wordpress.com/2018/10/30/macrostomum-seminal-fluid/) from their theses - but look out for more coming soon!
