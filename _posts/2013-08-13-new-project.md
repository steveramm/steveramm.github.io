I'm pleased to announce that my project proposal to study the function and evolution of seminal fluid in _Macrostomum_ has just been approved by the DFG (Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft). Over a 3-year research programme, we will use a combination of experimental and comparative work to establish the role that seminal fluid plays in this simultaneously hermaphroditic model system. Two PhD positions will be available as part of the project, and I'll be posting a formal advert very soon. In the meantime, informal expressions of interest from suitably qualified students (MSc required) are welcome…
