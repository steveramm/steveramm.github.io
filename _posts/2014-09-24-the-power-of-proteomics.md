_[B](http://beheco.oxfordjournals.org)_[_ehavioral Ecology_](http://beheco.oxfordjournals.org) recently published an excellent Invited Review by [Cristina-Maria Valcu](http://www.orn.mpg.de/409052/employee_page?c=671278&employee_id=27541) and [Bart Kempenaers](http://www.orn.mpg.de/2622/Department_Kempenaers) of the Max Planck Institute for Ornithology on "[Proteomics in behavioral ecology](http://beheco.oxfordjournals.org/content/early/2014/06/09/beheco.aru096.abstract)". Their review will eventually be accompanied by a number of Invited Commentaries, including [this one by me](http://dx.doi.org/10.1093/beheco/aru167).
