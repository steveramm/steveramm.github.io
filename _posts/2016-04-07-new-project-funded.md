Huge congratulations to [Yumi Nakadera](http://www.uni-bielefeld.de/\(en\)/biologie/Evolutionsbiologie/mitarbeiter/nakadera.html), just awarded a [Humboldt Research Fellowship](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/web/start.html) to study the adaptive dynamics of seminal fluid in snails. The project will allow Yumi to continue developing her independent research programme over the next two years; it will be hosted in my lab here in Bielefeld, with collaboration partners in [Amsterdam](http://www.joriskoene.com) and [Rennes](http://www6.rennes.inra.fr/ese_eng/PEOPLE/Personne_umr/\(idpers\)/203/\(idlang\)/uk).
