After a long gestation and a big team effort from colleagues across four countries, delighted to see this one finally out! We used RNA-Seq to probe the genetic basis of sex allocation plasticity in _Macrostomum lignano_, and then performed follow-up investigations of several plastic transcripts using _in situ_ hybridisation and RNA interference. The main result is that a large proportion of the _M. lignano_ transcriptome - and especially those transcripts functionally implicated in one or the other sex function - is differentially expressed according to the social environment, in line with expectations from sex allocation theory. You can read more about the research in this [Interview](https://molecularecologyblog.com/2019/06/21/interview-with-the-author-sex-allocation-plasticity-on-a-transcriptome-scale-socially-sensitive-gene-expression-in-a-simultaneous-hermaphrodite/) for [Molecular Ecology Spotlight](https://molecularecologyblog.com), or read the paper itself [here](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.15077).
