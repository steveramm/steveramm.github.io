[![Spermatozoons\_42]({{ '/assets/images/sperm-competition-special-issue/spermatozoons_42.png' | relative_url }})]({{ '/assets/images/sperm-competition-special-issue/spermatozoons_42.png' | relative_url }}) The journal [_Molecular Human Reproduction_](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org) has just published a special series on the theme of sperm competition, which the editor-in-chief [Chris Barratt](http://medicine.dundee.ac.uk/staff-member/professor-christopher-barratt) and I devised as a way of trying to bridge the divide between evolutionary and biomedical researchers interested in reproduction. I introduce the series in [this Editorial](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org/content/early/2014/10/16/molehr.gau076.full) and there are four reviews in total: [Jussi Lehtonen](http://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=FAWAA-IAAAAJ&hl=fi) and [Geoff Parker](http://scholar.google.de/citations?user=1JmtIa8AAAAJ&hl=en) writing on the [evolution of anisogamy](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org/content/early/2014/10/16/molehr.gau068.abstract), a review I wrote together with [Lukas Schärer](http://evolution.unibas.ch/scharer/), [Jens Ehmcke](https://www.uni-muenster.de/forschungaz/person/12776;jsessionid=8a9fdc9b771eb1c307bc76fd74c5) and [Joachim Wistuba](https://www.uni-muenster.de/forschungaz/person/12065;jsessionid=e49df3ee757188cf84f02dca0cfe) on the [evolution of spermatogenesis](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org/content/early/2014/10/16/molehr.gau070.abstract), [John Fitzpatrick](http://www.manchester.ac.uk/research/john.fitzpatrick/) and [Stefan Lüpold](http://pitnicklab.syr.edu/stefan/) writing about the [evolution of sperm quality](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org/content/early/2014/10/16/molehr.gau067.abstract) and [Ellen Kosman](http://www.bio.fsu.edu/grad_research3.php#K) and [Don Levitan](http://www.bio.fsu.edu/faculty-levitan.php) reviewing the [evolution of gametic compatibility](http://molehr.oxfordjournals.org/content/early/2014/10/16/molehr.gau069.abstract). Thanks to all involved - enjoy!

Kosman ET, Levitan DR. 2014.
