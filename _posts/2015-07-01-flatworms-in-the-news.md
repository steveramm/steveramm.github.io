It's not every day that _Macrostomum_ research makes the news, but I guess with [this particular paper](https://macrostomum.wordpress.com/2015/07/01/self-injecting-sperm/) - given the subject-matter - it was a pretty safe bet. Still, delighted to see quite wide coverage of our findings in the media today, including these stories:

“[Flatworm uses 'hypodermic penis' to inject sperm into own head](http://www.theguardian.com/science/2015/jul/01/flatworm-uses-hypodermic-penis-to-inject-sperm-into-own-head)” **The Guardian** (UK)

[“Who's Your Daddy? This Worm Injects Itself With Sperm to Get Pregnant”](http://www.nbcnews.com/science/weird-science/whos-your-daddy-mommy-worm-can-inject-itself-sperm-get-n384711) **NBC News** (US)

[“These flatworms plunge their penises into their own heads to inject themselves with sperm (when they must).”](http://blogs.discovermagazine.com/science-sushi/2015/06/30/flatworm-hypodermic-self-insemination-by-injecting-sperm-into-their-own-heads/) **Discover Magazine** (US)

["Lonely flatworms inject sperm into their own heads"](http://news.sciencemag.org/plants-animals/2015/07/lonely-flatworms-inject-sperm-their-own-heads?rss=1) **Science** (AAAS, US)

["Flatworm can self-fertilize by stabbing itself in the head"](https://www.sciencenews.org/blog/science-ticker/flatworm-can-self-fertilize-stabbing-itself-head?tgt=nr) **Science News** (SSP, US)

["This worm stabs its own sperm into its head to reproduce"](http://www.wired.co.uk/news/archive/2015-07/01/flatworm-immaculate-conception) **Wired** (UK)

[“Plattwurm schießt sich Spermien in den Kopf”](http://www.wissenschaft.de/home/-/journal_content/56/12054/7131550/Plattwurm-schießt-sich-Spermien-in-den-Kopf/) **Bild der Wissenschaft** (Germany)

["Ein besonders bizarrer Fall tierischer Selbstbesamung"](http://derstandard.at/2000018335627/Ein-besonders-bizarrer-Fall-tierischer-Selbstbesamung) **Der Standard** (Austria)

["Researchers discover hermaphrodite worm that uses 'needle-like penis' to shoot sperm into its own HEAD if it can't find a mate"](http://www.dailymail.co.uk/sciencetech/article-3146260/Researchers-discover-hermaphrodite-worm-uses-needle-like-penis-shoot-sperm-HEAD-t-mate.html) **Daily Mail** (UK)

There's also a press release about the research available on the Uni Basel website, published in English and German as “[The Bizarre Mating Habits of Flatworms](https://www.unibas.ch/en/News-Events/News/Uni-Research/The-Bizarre-Mating-Habits-of-Flatworms.html)” and “[Die seltsamen Sexualpraktiken des Plattwurms](https://www.unibas.ch/de/Aktuell/News/Uni-Research/Die-seltsamen-Sexualpraktiken-des-Plattwurms.html)”.

And the results seem to be attracting a fair amount of comment on social media - much of it unprintable here! (The IFLS post on Facebook, for example - web version [here](http://www.iflscience.com/plants-and-animals/flatworm-reproduces-using-its-penis-inject-sperm-its-head) -  had gathered >85k likes and >10k comments in just the first few hours.)
