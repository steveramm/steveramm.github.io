Following Elena's brilliant evidence synthesis Master thesis project last year, it's a pleasure to welcome Sébastien Bruzzo as the first M2 student to conduct an experimental project in the new lab. Sébastien's 6-month internship will focus on the mechanistic basis of heat and salinity-induced insults to fertility. In particular, we want to test how chronic and acute stressors (singly or in combination) affect flatworm fertility; whether those have differential impacts on the male or female sex function; and examine some of the key traits that are likely to mediate those responses, such as sperm morphology and mating behaviour.
