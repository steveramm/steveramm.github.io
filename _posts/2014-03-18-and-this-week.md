...sees the start of our new DFG research project “[Functional and Evolutionary Genetics of Seminal Fluid](http://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/240721607)”, the proposal for which arose from the initial [Nachwuchsakademie OFFSPRing](http://www.dfg.de/foerderung/info_wissenschaft/archiv/2012/info_wissenschaft_12_20/index.html) activities aimed at junior researchers across a diverse range of disciplines whose work falls within the remit of the DFG subject area “Reproductive Medicine & Biology”. A very warm welcome to the two new PhD students on the project, Bahar and Michael!
