[Athina](https://cmsedit.uni-bielefeld.de/fakultaeten/biologie/forschung/arbeitsgruppen/evo_biology/team/index.xml)'s new paper on variation in waiting times to selfing among _Macrostomum hystrix_ populations has [been published](https://doi.org/10.1080/07924259.2020.1732485) in _Invertebrate Reproduction and Development_. Based on our [initial observations](http://dx.doi.org/10.1111/jeb.12012) of worms from Pisa, we would have labelled _M. hystrix_ as a preferentially outcrossing species, but Athina's results analysing worms from Lignano Sabbiadoro suggest that at least some natural populations are readily selfing. This makes _M. hystrix_ a fascinating model system to try and tease apart the causes and consequences of within- and between-population variation in selfing propensity and to study mating system transitions.
