  <section class="page-grid research-item">
    <header class="page-label">
      <span class="section-number">01.02</span>
      <h2>Superfluidity</h2>
      <p class="meta">Quantum matter</p>
    </header>
    <div class="page-content">
      <img
        src="{{ '/images/research/superfluidity.svg' | relative_url }}"
        class="research-figure"
        width="400"
        height="200"
        loading="lazy"
        alt="Illustration of superfluid helium">
      <p>
        Quantum mechanical explanation of the behavior of liquid
        helium near absolute zero. Using path integral methods,
        we developed a microscopic theory of the lambda transition
        and explained the energy spectrum of excitations in
        superfluid helium, including the roton minimum.
      </p>

  <section class="page-grid research-item">
    <header class="page-label">
      <span class="section-number">01.03</span>
      <h2>Parton Model</h2>
      <p class="meta">Particle physics</p>
    </header>
    <div class="page-content">
      <img
        src="{{ '/images/research/partons.svg' | relative_url }}"
        class="research-figure"
        width="400"
        height="200"
        loading="lazy"
        alt="Illustration of the parton model">
      <p>
        A model describing the internal structure of hadrons in
        terms of point-like constituents called partons. This
        framework proved essential for interpreting deep inelastic
        scattering experiments at SLAC and laid the groundwork
        for quantum chromodynamics (QCD).
      </p>
