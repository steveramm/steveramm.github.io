<div class="page-grid research-item"> <header class="page-label"> <p class="section-number">01.01</p> <h2>Climate change and fertility</h2> </header>

<div class="page-content"> <p>The ability to maintain fertility under environmental stress is a key determinant of population persistence. However, we still lack a predictive framework for how reproductive function evolves under environmental change, limiting our ability to forecast adaptive potential in natural populations. We're using both single-generation and experimental evolution approaches to better understand the capacity of organisms to adapt to chronic and acute climate change stressors - with a current emphasis on temperature and salinity impacts in Macrostomum flatworms - alongside various meta-analysis projects to establish general principles of climate change adaptation.
</p> </div> </div>
