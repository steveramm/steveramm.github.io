<div class="page-grid research-intro"> <div class="page-label"></div>

<div class="page-content"> <p class="research-lead">My teaching philosophy is to prioritise concepts over facts, and to encourage
students to engage with not just scientific knowledge but also the process of
producing it. I want people to graduate with a broadly-applicable education that trains analytical and
communication skills alongside a thorough understanding of biology.</p> </div> </div>

<div class="page-grid research-item"> <header class="page-label"> <p class="section-number">01.01</p> <h2>Master EFCE</h2> </header>

<div class="page-content"> <p>I'm jointly responsible for the Master BEE programme EFCE (functional, behavioural and evolutionary ecology) co-accredited by the Université de Rennes and Institut Agro Rennes Angers. Entry to M1 is via the usual Mon Master application procedure; the M2 cohort (35-40 students per year) comprises those validating M1 EFCE, engineers from Institut Agro choosing EFCE for their final-year specialisation and potentially a small number of direct applicants to the University (via the annual campaign here). If you're interested in joining the programme in either M1 or M2, feel free to contact me to discuss the programme contents or entry routes.
</p> </div> </div>

<div class="page-grid research-item"> <header class="page-label"> <p class="section-number">01.02</p> <h2>Current teaching</h2> </header>

<div class="page-content"> <p>For <strong>M2 EFCE/IMABEE</strong>:
<br>
BEL (Evolution of behavior and life history traits): lectures on fundamentals of evolution and genetics applied to behaviour and life history; trade-offs and correlations in evolution; sexual and genetic conflicts; social evolution and major evolutionary transitions.
<br>
GSF (Getting Science Funded): lectures and practicals on responding to a funding call - including setting objectives, experimental design, staff and budget planning - with the aim of developing writing and communication skills. 
<br>
RET (Review Thesis): together with Philippe Vandenkoornhuyse, we coordinate this literature review module during the first semester that aims to prepare students to embark on their research internship in semester 2.
<br>
STAGE (Research Internship): together with Julien Petillon, we are responsible for coordinating the 6-month M2 internship, validating topics, tutoring students (together with Manuel Plantegenest) and organising the thesis defenses each June.</p>
<br>
<p>For <strong>M1 EFCE, IMABEE and MODE</strong>, I teach on the modules:
<br>
FECO (Fondements écologiques): lectures on evolutionary fundamentals and sexual selection; practical on game theory.
<br>
PP1 (Projet phase 1): supervising group projects at the Paimpont Biological Field Station.
<br>
PP2 (Projet phase 2): supervising group projects with a more molecular/analysis component.</p>
<br>
<p>For the licence (Bachelor) programme <strong>BO3E</strong>, I teach on the modules:
<br>
EG1 (General Ecology): lectures and practicals on population dynamics. 
<br>
EV2 (Approfondissement en évolution): lectures and practicals on genome evolution and molecular biology.
<br>
RED (Rédaction scientifique): lectures and practicals on scientific writing.
</p> </div> </div>
