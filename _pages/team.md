<div class="team-member"> <h2 class="team-member-name">Steve Ramm</h2> <p> <img src="/images/steve_not_cropped.jpg" alt="Steve Ramm" class="team-photo"> Principal Investigator. Bio <a href="/about/">here</a>. </p> <div style="clear: both;"></div> </div>

<div class="team-member"> <h2 class="team-member-name">Quentin Corbel</h2> <p> <img src="/images/team/quentin.jpg" alt="Quentin Corbel" class="team-photo"> Postdoc. Quentin is working on the ANR project FRIDA, in collaboration with Sylvain Glémin at Ecobio and collaborators in Montpellier, Perpignan and Lyon. Together we are interested in using two selfing animal species to decipher the relative contributions of genetic and epigenetic effects to a fundamental phenomenon in evolutionary biology, inbreeding depression. Quentin joins us following a recent postdoc at CNRS Moulis and a PhD at the University of Valencia on adaptive reproductive plasticity and life-history trade-offs in _Drosophila_. </p> <div style="clear: both;"></div> </div>

<div class="team-member"> <h2 class="team-member-name">Elise Jeanne</h2> <p> <img src="/images/team/elise.jpg" alt="Elise Jeanne" class="team-photo"> PhD student. As part of the project “Fertility in a Changing World”, Elise is investigating plastic and evolutionary responses of flatworms to altered thermal and salinity environments. Her project aims to clarify sex differences in environmentally-imposed fertility limits and test the interaction between natural and sexual selection in driving adaptation to climate change.</p> <div style="clear: both;"></div> </div>

<div class="team-member"> <h2 class="team-member-name">Sarthak Grover</h2> <p> <img src="/images/team/sarthak.jpg" alt="Sarthak grover" class="team-photo"> PhD student. In his project “Selfing Under Stress”, Sarthak is investigating potential links between environmental variation, inbreeding depression and mating system expression. Sarthak initially joined the lab in Bielefeld in 2022 while he was a BS/MS student at IISER Mohali, completing his Master thesis research on mating system expression and sex allocation in the flatworm _Macrostomum hystricinum_. </p> <div style="clear: both;"></div> </div>

<div class="team-member"> <h2 class="team-member-name">Astrid Bavay</h2> <p> Technician. Working for the Ecolex technical platform at ECOBIO, Astrid is helping us to maintain our flatworm and algae cultures. </p> <div style="clear: both;"></div> </div>

<div class="page-grid research-item"> <header class="page-label"> <h2>Alumni</h2> </header> <div class="page-content"> 

Kélian Salvadego – M2 intern (2026). A student on the EFCE Master programme in Rennes, Kélian worked with Sarthak to quantify the relationship between environmental stress and inbreeding depression, and then stayed on to help us in the lab maintaining stocks and experimental cultures over the summer. 
<br><br>
Léo Jamon – M1 intern (2026). A student on the EFCE Master programme in Rennes, Léo worked with Elise to study the experimental evolution of thermal adaptation.
<br><br>
Clara Crozatier – M1 intern (2026). A student on the BEE Master programme at Aix Marseille Université, Clara joined the lab to work with Elise on the contributions of natural and sexual selection to adaptation under novel thermal and salinity regimes.
<br><br>
Shaman Dor-Vincent – M1 intern (2026). A student on the IMABEE Master programme in Rennes, Shaman investigated the molecular basis of sex allocation plasticity in Macrostomum lignano.
<br><br>
Sébastien Bruzzo – M2 intern (2025). Sébastien performed experiments to understand the mechanistic basis of heat and salinity-induced insults to fertility. His project tested how chronic and acute stressors (singly or in combination) affect flatworm fertility; whether those have differential impacts on the male or female sex function; and examine some of the key traits that are likely to mediate those responses, such as sperm morphology and mating behaviour. Currently being written up!
<br><br>
Lucas Bernard – M1 intern (2025). A student on the EFCE Master programme in Rennes, Lucas worked with Sébastien and Elise on experiments to understand the mechanistic basis of heat and salinity-induced insults to fertility.
<br><br>
Eva Esteve – M1 intern (2025). Joining us from the Ecophysiology, Ecology and Ethology Master at Uni Strasbourg, Eva worked on climate change and fertility, with a particular focus on sperm-mediated impacts of heat stress.
<br><br>
Emma Lavaut – (2024-2025) as an LRU lecturer-researcher at ECOBIO, Emma was interested in how environmental stressors affect the operation of sexual selection, and how this can be quantified using Bateman gradients and other metrics.
<br><br>
Elena Breitenberger – M2 intern (2024). Elena used meta-analysis (in collaboration with Liam Dougherty in Liverpool) to investigate multi-stressor impacts on animal fertility, with an eventual focus on salinity that found some really strong and important effects – currently being written up for publication!
<br><br>
Sonja Schindler – Biological-Technical Assistant in the Department of Evolutionary Biology, Bielefeld University, Sonja was instrumental in maintaining or worm cultures and supporting various research projects in Bielefeld.
<br><br>
Athina Giannakara – as a Master student (2013-15), Research Assistant (2016-17), PhD student (2018-2020) and Postdoc (2021-2023), Athina contributed to many different projects and was a key member of the group for the 10+ years we were in Bielefeld. After working on Macrostomum spermatogenesis for her Master thesis project, she contributed to some other projects in the lab (e.g. here, here and here) before completing her PhD on the evolution of self-fertilisation in Macrostomum flatworms, including M. pusillum and M. hystrix. Finally, she worked as a postdoc on the function and evolution of seminal fluid proteins.
<br><br>
Suhaas Sehgal – a visiting BS/MS student from IISER Mohali, Suhaas joined the lab initially with an undergraduate project scholarship funded by the Association for the Study of Animal Behaviour to study resource-dependent sex allocation in Macrostomum lignano, and then conducted his Master thesis research on seminal fluid-mediated effects. Suhaas is now a PhD student with John Pannell in Lausanne.
<br><br>
Shreya Dimri – Shreya completed her Master thesis (2021) working on the molecular basis of sex allocation plasticity, and is now a PhD student in the Department of Evolutionary Biology, Bielefeld University, working on a meta-analysis project with Alfredo Sanchez-Tojar and Klaus Reinhold to understand phenotypic variation within and between species.
<br><br>
Michael Weber – Postdoc (2019-2020) and PhD student (2014-2019). Michael completed his PhD on the identification, functional characterisation and fitness effects of seminal fluid proteins in Macrostomum in early 2019, and was then awarded a Career Bridge Postdoctoral Fellowship to work on environmental impacts on fertility.
<br><br>
Bahar Patlar – PhD student (2014-2018). Bahar completed her PhD on the evolutionary quantitative genetics of seminal fluid in Macrostomum, including describing genetic and environmental sources of variation in seminal fluid expression, identifying functions of specific seminal fluid proteins and investigating genotype x environment effects on sperm competitive ability. Following postdoctoral fellowships in Alberto Civetta‘s lab at the University of Winnipeg and Amro Zayed’s lab at York University in Toronto, Canada, Bahar is now a research associate (group leader) in the Department of Animal Ecology at the University of Halle-Wittenberg, Germany.
<br><br>
Yumi Nakadera – Postdoc (2015-2019). Yumi joined the department as one of Bielefeld University’s career bridge fellows, and was then awarded a Postdoctoral Research Fellowship from the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation, working on seminal fluid evolution in snails, and especially on plasticity in seminal fluid expression and seeking evidence for rapid functional divergence between populations. She is now a postdoc in Joris Koene‘s lab at VU Amsterdam.
<br><br>
Lennart Winkler – Bachelor student (2015-2016), Student assistant (2016-2017), Master student (2017-2019). Lennart completed his Bachelor thesis on sex allocation in Macrostomum hystrix and his Master thesis on the evolution of the house mouse baculum (in collaboration with Anna Lindholm, University of Zurich). He is now a PhD student at TU Dresden (with Tim Janicke and Klaus Reinhardt).
<br><br>
Maike Foraita – Master student (2017) jointly supervised with Klaus Reinhold. Maike’s thesis investigated the sperm removal behaviour of the bush cricket Metaplastes ornatus (published here). Maike is now pursuing a PhD on animal behaviour and cognition at La Trobe University, Australia.
<br><br>
Mark Koschmieder – Master student (2017) jointly supervised with Matthias Galipaud. Mark’s Master thesis modelled the evolution of allometry under sexual selection.
